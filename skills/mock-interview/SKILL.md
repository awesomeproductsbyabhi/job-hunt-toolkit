# Mock interview

Load the playbook (answer rules, rubric, mock protocol), the story bank and the company's prep doc first. Judge answers only against the story bank, the career notes and the resume.

## Setup
Ask only what is unknown: the round and its length, the interviewer's likely profile, and a short (screen) or full run.

## Running the round
1. Ask one question at a time, as a real interviewer would, including follow-ups on numbers, ownership and gaps, and one unexpected question. No hints while the user answers.
2. The user answers in full, aloud or typed, and times it.
3. After each answer, score 1 to 5 on seven points: answers the question, structure, specificity and numbers, ownership (I versus we), honesty, brevity and delivery, fit to the company and role. Quote the weakest sentence, give one fix, and offer a cleaner version of that part only.
4. Check every number against the numbers sheet. Flag any estimate, plan or open figure and any unsupported claim.
5. At the end, summarise scores by criterion, the two weakest answers and the one habit to fix first.
6. Repeat the two lowest questions until each scores 4 or more. For product or payments cases, also score framing, assumptions, metrics, trade-offs, risks and a clear recommendation.
7. The last mock before the real interview runs the exact length and format without pauses.

## After
Propose, for the user's confirmation, changes to a story card, the prep doc or a new learnings-log rule. Do not save without confirmation. Log the mock date, questions and scores in the prep doc so the debrief can compare mock with real.
