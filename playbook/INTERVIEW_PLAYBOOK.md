# Interview playbook (template)

A process for preparing, practising and learning from interviews. Fill the story bank and learnings log with your own material; none is included here.

## 1. Process
Confirm details, run the kickoff skill, build a prep page per round, mock the two weakest answers, interview, debrief within two hours, update the log.

## 2. Timeline
Prep starts when a company replies. Under 48 hours: review the numbers sheet, the three lead stories and the standard answers, then one timed mock.

## 3. Answer rules
- Answer the question first, then give the story.
- Problem or analysis, then action, then a numeric result.
- Say "I" for your own work and "we" for the team; credit others accurately.
- Say "about" for estimates; label plans as plans.
- No partner or customer names. Keep answers at the gist level, not process detail.

## 4. Scoring rubric and mock protocol
Score each answer 1 to 5 on: answers the question, structure, specificity and numbers, ownership, honesty, brevity and delivery, fit to the role. Repeat the two lowest until each reaches 4. The last mock runs the real length without pauses.

## 5. Intel checklist
Job description themes, company and product news, competitors, reported interview questions (tag each "reported, unverified" with source and date).

## 6. Debrief template
Facts; every question in order; gist of each answer; stumbles; what landed; role signals; surprises; items to double-check; next steps; self-score.

## 7. Easy-to-miss items
Notice period, reason for leaving, pay approach, years of experience, location, team size led. Keep one standard answer for each and reuse it everywhere.

## 8. Fit brief and offer readiness
Before each interview, write a short fit brief: why this role, strongest match, biggest gap and how to address it. Decide your offer criteria before an offer arrives.
